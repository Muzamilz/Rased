## 2. The 90-Day Plan

### Days 1–10: Validate the pain before writing more code
- Message 15–20 HR leads at UAE companies (50–300 employees) on LinkedIn — see outreach
  script below
- Goal: 5+ real 15-minute calls
- In each call, ask (don't pitch first):
  - "How do you currently track your Emiratization compliance?"
  - "Have you ever been surprised by a fine or a quota-band change?"
  - "What would it be worth to you to know your risk 3 months before a deadline?"
- **Do not build anything else until you've done at least 5 of these calls.** This is the
  highest-leverage thing you can do this week.

### Days 10–20: Build the MVP
- Use the TRD + OpenCode workflow above
- Build only what's in "Must Have" in the PRD
- Validate the rules engine against at least 3 manually-checked examples

### Days 20–30: Get it in front of the people you talked to
- Go back to your 5 interviewees: "I built the thing we talked about — 10 minutes to show
  you?"
- Watch them use it. Don't explain — watch where they get confused.
- Ask directly: "Would you pay for this if it connected live to your MOHRE data?"

### Days 30–60: Iterate + find 1 real design partner
- Fix what's broken based on real usage
- Get one company to use it with real (even if partial) data on an ongoing basis
- Start the conversation about what a paid pilot would look like

### Days 60–90: Build your story + apply
- Write the pitch deck from real user quotes and real usage, not hypotheticals
- Apply to YC (or approach regional accelerators — see below) with a working product and
  real validation, not just a slide deck

## 3. Outreach Script (Days 1–10)

**LinkedIn message template:**

> Hi [Name] — I'm building a tool to help UAE HR teams see their Emiratization compliance
> risk before it becomes a fine, not after. Would you be open to a 15-min call to tell me how
> you currently handle this? Not selling anything — just trying to understand the real
> problem before I build the wrong thing.

Keep it short. No pitch deck attached. The goal of this message is a *conversation*, not a
sale.

## 4. Realistic Talent/Resource Gaps (Be Honest With Yourself)

As a solo founder, you don't have:
- A native Arabic speaker to QA language quality (find one — even a freelancer for a few
  hours to review your reports before showing users)
- HR/legal domain expertise to validate the compliance math (find an HR consultant willing
  to sanity-check your rules engine for a coffee/favor — this is worth more than any code
  you write)
- Government relationships (don't chase this yet — it comes after you have traction, not
  before)

**Your job in the first 90 days is not to build a complete product. It's to prove one
person with real pain is willing to change their behavior because of what you built.**

---

## 5. Where to Take This After MVP Validation

- **Regional accelerators to consider alongside YC:** Hub71 (Abu Dhabi), Flat6Labs, Dtec
  (Dubai) — worth checking current programs, as GCC accelerator offerings change often and
  are worth verifying directly before assuming details
- **YC application:** they care most about (1) a sharp problem statement, (2) evidence
  someone besides you cares, (3) you personally being relentless about talking to users.
  A working demo + 5 real user conversations + 1 design partner beats a polished deck with
  no users, every time.

---

## 6. What NOT to Do in the Next 90 Days

- Don't build Saudi support yet
- Don't build accounts/login yet
- Don't chase government API access yet — you have no leverage for that conversation until
  you have users
- Don't incorporate a company yet unless a real pilot deal requires it — that's a legal/cost
  decision to make once there's real signal, not before
- Don't over-engineer the architecture — the TRD above is intentionally minimal for a reason
